Clean Room by Tippy Games

Clean Room has no accounts of its own and keeps no data on our servers. Your game data can be in two places, and you can delete both yourself, at any time.

Progress saved on your phone

Your progress and settings are saved only on your phone. To delete them, do any one of these:

  1. In the game: New game → Start over deletes your saved progress.
  2. On your phone: Settings → Apps → Clean Room → Storage → Clear data.
  3. Uninstall the game.

Cloud save in Google Play Games

If you signed in with Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is kept in your Play Games profile so you can continue on another phone. To delete it:

  1. Open the Google Play Games app, or the Play Store and tap your profile picture → Play Games.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete Play Games account & data.
  3. Under Delete individual game data, find Clean Room and tap Delete.

This deletes the cloud save right away. Google's instructions: Delete your Play Games data or Play Games profile.

Ask us

If you can't do it yourself, email tippygamesstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Clean Room data deletion". We hold no data about you, so we'll walk you through the steps above, usually within a few days.
