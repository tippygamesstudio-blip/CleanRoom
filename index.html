CLEAN ROOM

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Clean Room ("the game") is made by Tippy Games ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

Your progress (your choices and where you are in the story) and your settings, such as sound on or off, are saved only on your phone. We can't see them. They are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Conversations in the game

Every message, character and conversation in Clean Room is part of the story. The game does not read your real messages, contacts, photos or calls, and you can't contact other players through it.

Permissions

The game asks for internet access only because Unity, the engine it is built with, includes it by default. The current version does not use the internet to send any information about you.

The game can also make your phone vibrate when a message arrives in the story. You can turn this off in the game's Settings.

Google Play

You download the game from Google Play, which has its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example, saving your progress to your Google Play Games account so you can continue on another phone, or buying later seasons through Google Play), we will update this policy and the effective date before that version is released.
